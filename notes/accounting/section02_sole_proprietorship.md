# Section 2: Sole Proprietorship — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 3-4 pts | 8 past papers extracted**

> **Key formulas:**
> - Owner's income = Own salary + Own interest +/- Profit or Loss
> - Closing equity = Opening equity + Equity increase - Private surplus (or + Private credit surplus) +/- Profit or Loss
> - Private account: Debit = withdrawals, captive consumption | Credit = salary, interest
> - Left-side surplus → owner took more than earned → `Equity / Private`
> - Right-side surplus → owner earned more than took → `Private / Equity`

## Q1: January 2026 (3 pts) — Guido Grimm

**Given data:**

| Item | CHF |
|------|-----|
| Equity at the beginning of the year | 100 000 |
| Increase in equity during the year | 10 000 |
| Left-side surplus of the account "Private" at year end | 5 000 |
| Expenses for Trading Goods | 120 000 |
| Expenses for wages and salaries for *Guido Grimm* | 50 000 |
| Expenses for wages and salaries for *Peter Stadler* | 8 000 |
| Financial expenses (interest for bank loan) | 4 000 |
| Financial expenses (own interest) | 5 000 |
| Various expenses | 32 000 |
| Profit of the year | 17 200 |

**Questions:**
1. What is the equity at the end of the year after closing the accounts?
2. What is the owner's income?
3. By which amount (+ or -) does the owner's income change if the interest rate had been 6% instead of 5% for both the bank loan and own interest?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **122 200** → 100 000 + 10 000 - 5 000 + 17 200
2. **72 200** → 17 200 + 50 000 + 5 000 (profit + own salary + own interest)
3. **-1 000** → Higher bank interest reduces profit by 800; higher own interest increases add-back by 1 000 but also reduces profit by 1 000. Net: only the bank loan increase hurts → -1 000

</details>

## Q2: March 2025 (4 pts) — Paula Pinto

**Given data for business year 2024:**

| Item | CHF |
|------|-----|
| Salary considered for *Paula Pinto* | 56 000 |
| Interest considered for *Paula Pinto* | 4 000 |
| Total cash withdrawals by *Paula Pinto* | 63 000 |
| Profit of the year (from P&L) | 13 000 |
| Equity in the opening statement of financial positions | 190 000 |
| Additional equity paid in by *Paula Pinto* during the year | 20 000 |

For December, *Paula Pinto* considers a salary of CHF 5 000 (already included in the numbers above).

**Questions:**
1. What is the booking entry for December salary?
2. What is the booking entry for closing the account "Private"? (No other bookings apply)
3. What is the value of equity at the end of the business year 2024?
4. What is the income of *Paula Pinto* in the business year 2024?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Expenses for salaries / Private — 5 000**
2. **Equity / Private — 3 000** → Private: Debit 63 000 (withdrawals) vs Credit 56 000 + 4 000 = 60 000. Left-side surplus = 3 000
3. **220 000** → 190 000 + 20 000 + 13 000 - 3 000
4. **73 000** → 56 000 + 4 000 + 13 000

</details>

## Q3: January 2025 (4 pts) — Sven Eisenhut

**Given data:**

| Item | CHF |
|------|-----|
| Salary for owner | 110 000 |
| Interest for owner | 8 000 |
| Cash withdrawals by the owner | 60 000 |
| Equity increase during the year | 20 000 |
| Loss of the period | 32 000 |
| Equity in the closing statement | 210 000 |

**Questions:**
1. What is the equity at the beginning of the year?
2. What is the income of *Sven Eisenhut*?
3. *Sven Eisenhut* takes CHF 120 in cash for private purchase in the supermarket (already included above). What is the booking entry?
4. What is the year-end booking for the account "Private"?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **164 000** → 210 000 + 32 000 - 20 000 - 110 000 - 8 000 + 60 000
   *(Work backwards: Closing equity = OB + increase + Private credit surplus - loss)*
2. **86 000** → 110 000 + 8 000 - 32 000
3. **Private / Cash — 120**
4. **Private / Equity — 58 000** → Right-side surplus: (110 000 + 8 000) - 60 000 = 58 000

</details>

## Q5: March 2024 — Vanessa (booking entries)

**Provide booking entries for the sole proprietorship of Vanessa:**

1. In November, *Vanessa* pays CHF 55 000 as long-term contribution to the company's bank account.
2. The company considers a lease income of CHF 4 600 to Vanessa, for the apartment in the business building which she uses privately.
3. By year end, interest for equity of CHF 6 600 is considered for Vanessa.
4. As per December 31, the account "Private" has a left side surplus of CHF 9 500. What is the applicable booking?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Bank / Equity — 55 000**
2. **Private / Income from land and building — 4 600**
3. **Financial expenses / Private — 6 600**
4. **Equity / Private — 9 500** *(left-side surplus = owner took more than earned)*

</details>

**Questions:**

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Owner's income** = own salary + own interest +/- profit/loss (NOT cash withdrawals!)
- **Left-side surplus** on Private = owner withdrew more than earned → close with `Equity / Private`
- **Right-side surplus** on Private = owner earned more than withdrew → close with `Private / Equity`
- They sometimes sneak in an extra transaction AFTER giving you the "current surplus" — adjust!
- **Peter Stadler's salary** in Q1 is a distraction — only the OWNER's salary counts for owner's income
- Watch for "5% of equity" for own interest — you must calculate it yourself
