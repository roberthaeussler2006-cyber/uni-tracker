## PART B: T-ACCOUNT CALCULATION QUESTIONS

### Key Formulas

```
Purchase value of goods purchased = Sum of DEBIT side of "Expenses for trading goods"
                                    minus non-purchase debits (transport in, etc. if separated)

Purchase value of goods sold (PVGS) = Expenses for trading goods balance
                                       adjusted for inventory change
                                     = PVGP + Opening inventory - Closing inventory

Net Sales = Credit side of "Sales from trading goods" minus Debit side of "Sales from trading goods"

Gross Profit = Net Sales - PVGS

Gross Profit Margin = Gross Profit / Net Sales × 100

Gross Profit Mark-up = Gross Profit / PVGS × 100
```

3. **Gross Profit margin = 46.39%** → ((720 - 18.6) - 376) / (720 - 18.6) = 325.4 / 701.4
4. **Gross Profit mark-up = 86.54%** → ((720 - 18.6) - 376) / 376 = 325.4 / 376

## COMMON TRAPS & PATTERNS

### "No booking entry" traps (they LOVE testing this):
- **Ordering goods** (no delivery yet) → no booking
- **Replacing a defective item** with the same value → no booking
- **Sending a reminder** for overdue payment → no booking
- **Private purchases outside the business** (ordering from a different supplier for personal use) → no booking
- **Giving away goods for free** as goodwill (no invoice involved) → sometimes no booking

### Captive consumption (Private use):
- **ALWAYS at PURCHASE value**, never at selling price!
- Booking: `Private / Captive consumption — [purchase value]`
- They always give you both the selling and purchase price to trick you

### Trade discount vs Quantity discount:
- Trade discount (Skonto) = early payment discount, typically 2-5%
- Calculate discount on the REMAINING invoice amount (after any prior reductions)
- Two lines: `Payables / Expenses for trading goods — [discount amount]` AND `Payables / Bank — [net payment]`

### Transportation costs — SPLIT!
- **Freight IN** (supplier → shop) = `Expenses for trading goods / Payables`
- **Freight OUT** (shop → customer) = `Sales from trading goods / Payables`

### Non-trading purchases:
- Buying furniture, vehicles, machines → debit the ASSET account, not Expenses for trading goods!
- `Furniture and facilities / Payables` or `Vehicles / Payables` or `Machines / Payables`

### Inventory change at year-end:
- **Inventory DECREASED** (OB > CB): `Expenses for trading goods / Inventory, trading goods`
- **Inventory INCREASED** (CB > OB): `Inventory, trading goods / Expenses for trading goods`

### T-Account calculation steps:
1. **PVGP** = Sum of debits in "Expenses for trading goods" - Sum of credits + any unbooked items
2. **Inventory change** = Closing balance - Opening balance
3. **PVGS** = PVGP - inventory increase (or + inventory decrease)
4. **Net Sales** = Credit side of "Sales from trading goods" - Debit side
5. **Gross Profit** = Net Sales - PVGS
6. **Margin** = Gross Profit / Net Sales
7. **Mark-up** = Gross Profit / PVGS
