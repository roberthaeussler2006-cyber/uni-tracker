# Section 7: Effect on Profit and Cash — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 4 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Quick decision rules:**
> 1. Does the entry involve an **income or expense** account? → Profit affected (income = +, expense = -)
> 2. If BOTH income AND expense appear → they cancel out: Profit **0**
> 3. Does the entry involve **Cash or Bank**? → Cash affected (debit = +, credit = -)
> 4. If BOTH cash accounts appear (Bank/Cash) → they cancel out: Cash **0**
> 5. Marketable securities are sometimes cash equivalents → usually Cash **0** when swapped with Bank

## Patterns & Traps

- **Always 4 entries, 4 points** — one point per correct row
- **Expense + Income in same entry** → they cancel: profit **0** (e.g., Lease expenses / Income from land and building)
- **Bank / Cash** → both are cash, cancel out: cash **0**
- **Bank / Marketable securities** → usually treated as cash equivalents, so cash **0**
- **Crediting an expense account** = reducing expense = profit **+** (e.g., VA receivables / Losses from receivables)
- **Debiting an income account** = reducing income = profit **-** (e.g., Sales / Receivables for a return)
- **Provisions, Payables, Liabilities** → NOT cash accounts, so cash **0**
- **VA (value adjustment)** accounts are asset contra-accounts, NOT expense/income accounts themselves
