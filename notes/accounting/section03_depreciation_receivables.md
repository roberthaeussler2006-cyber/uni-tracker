# Section 3: Depreciation & Losses from Receivables — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 9-12 pts | 6 past papers extracted**

> **Key rules:**
> - Check if depreciation rate applies to **book value** or **purchase value** — they test this every time!
> - Direct method (no VA account): `Depreciation / Asset`
> - Indirect method (VA account): `Depreciation / VA, Asset`
> - Land + Building always uses `Expenses for Land and Building` (not "Depreciation")
> - Sending a **reminder** = NO booking entry
> - Starting debt collection = `Receivables / Bank` (pay fee)
> - Debt certificate repaid years later = `Bank / Extraordinary income`

## Q1: January 2026 — Depreciation Part A (3 pts)

**Non-current assets of Miller Ltd. (before depreciation):**

| Asset | Gross Value | VA | Book Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicles | 180 000 | -60 000 | 120 000 |
| Furniture and Facilities | 280 000 | — | 280 000 |
| Land + Building | 2 200 000 | -520 000 | 1 680 000 |

**Book the depreciation by year end:**
1. Vehicles depreciated at 20% of the **book value**
2. Furniture and Facilities depreciated at 10% of the **book value**
3. Land + Building depreciated at 1.5% of the **purchasing value**

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Depreciation / VA, Vehicles — 24 000** → 20% × 120 000
2. **Depreciation / Furniture and Facilities — 28 000** → 10% × 280 000 *(direct method — no VA account)*
3. **Expenses for Land + Building / VA, Land + Building — 33 000** → 1.5% × 2 200 000 *(purchasing value, not book value!)*

</details>

## Q2: January 2026 — Depreciation Part B (2 pts)

*Coros Ltd.* buys a new machine for CHF 400 000 at the beginning of 2026. Expected annual EBITDA: CHF 200 000. Other depreciation: CHF 50 000 annually. The new machine is depreciated starting 2026.

**What is the EBIT as per 2028 if the new machine is depreciated with...**

1. Straight line depreciation for 8 years?
2. Declining balance depreciation with 25% of book value?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

**Straight line:**
- Annual depreciation of machine: 400 000 / 8 = 50 000
- Total depreciation 2028: 50 000 (other) + 50 000 (machine) = 100 000
- EBIT = 200 000 - 100 000 = **100 000**

**Declining balance:**
- 2026: 25% × 400 000 = 100 000 → BV = 300 000
- 2027: 25% × 300 000 = 75 000 → BV = 225 000
- 2028: 25% × 225 000 = 56 250
- Total depreciation 2028: 50 000 + 56 250 = 106 250
- EBIT = 200 000 - 106 250 = **93 750**

</details>

## Q3: January 2026 — Losses from Receivables (7 pts)

**Provide booking entries from the perspective of Delivery Ltd.:**

1. Customer *Berta Bissig* has still not paid the overdue invoice of CHF 936, despite two reminders. To start a debt collection process, *Delivery Ltd.* pays CHF 54 by bank transfer to the government.

2. *FastMove Ltd.* is bankrupt. *Delivery Ltd.* has an overdue invoice of CHF 3 100, and had paid CHF 60 to start debt collection. Now receives a bank payment of CHF 1 200 and is informed that CHF 40 late payment interest applies, but no further payments will come.

3. *Delivery Ltd.* still has outstanding receivables of CHF 990 from *Berta Bissig* (see transaction 1). Today, the bank statement shows a payment of CHF 1 020 from her.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

**1.** Receivables from G&S / Bank — 54

**2.** Three entries:
- Receivables from G&S / Financial Income — 40 *(late interest)*
- Bank / Receivables from G&S — 1 200 *(payment received)*
- Losses from Receivables / Receivables from G&S — 2 000 *(write-off: 3 100 + 60 + 40 - 1 200)*

**3.** Two entries:
- Receivables from G&S / Financial Income — 30 *(late interest: 1 020 - 990)*
- Bank / Receivables from G&S — 1 020 *(full payment)*

</details>

## Q4: March 2025 — Part A: Depreciation (4 pts)

**Statement of financial positions of Turico Ltd. (before depreciation), in CHF:**

| Asset | Gross Value | VA | Book Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receivables from G&S | 28 000 | -1 600 | 26 400 |
| Machines | — | — | 269 000 |
| Furniture and facilities | 73 000 | -21 000 | 52 000 |
| Land and building | 500 000 | -90 000 | 410 000 |

**Book depreciation and losses from receivables at year end:**
1. Machines depreciated by 15% of the **book value**
2. Furniture and facilities depreciated by 10% of the **purchase value**
3. Land and building depreciated by 3% of the **book value**
4. Expected losses from receivables = 5% of total receivables from G&S

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Depreciation / Machines — 40 350** → 15% × 269 000 *(direct method)*
2. **Depreciation / VA, Furniture and Facilities — 7 300** → 10% × 73 000 *(purchase value!)*
3. **Expenses for Land and Building / VA, Land and Building — 12 300** → 3% × 410 000
4. **VA, Receivables from G&S / Losses from Receivables — 200** → Target: 5% × 28 000 = 1 400. Current VA: 1 600. VA too high by 200 → **reverse/reduce** *(tricky — VA decreases!)*

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

**Depreciation:**
- **Book value vs purchase value** — READ CAREFULLY every time. They mix these in the same question.
- Direct method (no VA): `Depreciation / Asset` — used when no VA account exists for that asset
- Indirect method (VA exists): `Depreciation / VA, Asset`
- Land + Building: ALWAYS `Expenses for Land and Building / VA, Land and Building` (never "Depreciation")
- Selling an asset above book value: split into sale entry + profit (Extraordinary income)

**Losses from receivables:**
- Reminder = **NO booking entry** (trap!)
- Debt collection fee: `Receivables from G&S / Bank`
- Successful collection: book late interest first (`Receivables / Financial Income`), then bank receipt
- Partial/failed collection: write off remainder as `Losses from Receivables / Receivables`
- Debt certificate repaid years later: `Bank / Extraordinary Income`
- Convert receivable to loan: `Loans granted / Receivables from G&S`
- Year-end VA adjustment: compare target VA (% × gross receivables) to current VA, book difference
  - VA too low → increase: `Losses from Receivables / VA, Receivables`
  - VA too high → decrease: `VA, Receivables / Losses from Receivables` (credit to losses!)
