# Section 14: P&L Reporting Format — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 4 pts (11 pts in Jun 2024) | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Earnings levels (top to bottom):**
> 1. **Gross Profit** = Net sales - Cost of goods (material, trading goods, changes in inventory)
> 2. **EBITDA** = Gross profit - Operating expenses (personnel, other operating, admin) + Other operating income
> 3. **EBIT** = EBITDA - Depreciation
> 4. **EBT** = EBIT + Financial result + Extraordinary items + Non-operating
> 5. **Profit of the year** = EBT - Taxes
>
> **Logic**: If a booking entry involves accounts within a level → it affects that level. If accounts are below or outside that level → no effect.

## Q1: January 2026 (4 pts)

What is the effect of the following booking entries onto the respective earnings level?

| Booking Entry | Earnings Level | Increase / No effect / Decrease? |
|---|---|---|
| Bank Account / Vehicles | EBT | ? |
| Expenses for Material / Payables for G&S | EBITDA | ? |
| Sales Trading Goods / Receivables from G&S | Gross Profit | ? |
| Marketable Securities / Financial Income | EBT | ? |

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

| Booking Entry | Level | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Bank / Vehicles | EBT | **No effect** *(asset swap, no P&L account)* |
| Expenses for Material / Payables for G&S | EBITDA | **Decrease** *(material expense increases → reduces EBITDA)* |
| Sales Trading Goods / Receivables from G&S | Gross Profit | **Decrease** *(debiting sales = reducing revenue → reduces gross profit)* |
| Marketable Securities / Financial Income | EBT | **Increase** *(financial income is above EBT line → increases EBT)* |

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **EBITDA trap**: Depreciation is BELOW EBITDA — it only affects EBIT and below
- **Extraordinary items**: Usually below EBIT, sometimes below EBT (depends on exam's P&L structure)
- **Land & building income/expenses**: Non-operating, so below EBIT
- **Financial income/expenses**: Between EBIT and EBT
- **Equity bookings** (profit distribution, profit brought forward): No P&L effect at all
- **Asset swaps** (Bank/Vehicles): No P&L effect
- **Debiting a revenue account** = reducing revenue = DECREASE
- **Crediting an expense account** = reducing expense = INCREASE
