## Q1: January 2026 (4 pts — 6 questions)

**1.** Which measure increases the return on equity?
- **Answer**: Adding more liabilities, if the interest is lower than the return on assets *(leverage effect)*

**2.** Which situation applies if liabilities are significantly lower than assets?
- **Answer**: None of the above applies

**3.** A company sells its old production machines and sends an invoice. Transaction type?
- **Answer**: Asset swap *(machine → receivable)*

**4.** Computer for CHF 4 800, residual value CHF 0 after 5 years. Best depreciation method?
- **Answer**: Straight-line depreciation

**5.** AI in accounting — which statement does NOT apply?
- **Answer**: AI will likely replace accountants

**6.** Modern accounting software — which statement does NOT apply?
- **Answer**: Modern accounting software allows writing supplier invoices and automates expense bookings

## Q2: March 2025 (6 pts — 6 questions)

**1.** In a sole proprietorship, which account for owner taking goods for private use?
- **Answer**: Account "Captive consumption"

**2.** Statement regarding account "Private" that applies?
- **Answer**: If at year end "Private" shows a right-side surplus, this leads to an increase of "Equity"

**3.** FaPa Ltd. sends debt collection fee to government by bank transfer. Transaction type?
- **Answer**: Asset swap *(bank → receivable from debt collection)*

**4.** When assets no longer cover liabilities + at least 50% of equity?
- **Answer**: Loss of capital *(Kapitalverlust)*

**5.** Trading company wants to increase Quick Ratio. Which measure does NOT help?
- **Answer**: Increase trade discount from 2% to 4% on receivables (customers pay faster)

**6.** Expected payment defaults = 5% of receivables. Opening receivables CHF 100 000, closing CHF 80 000. How does VA balance change?
- **Answer**: Balance decreases by CHF 1 000 *(Opening VA: 5% × 100 000 = 5 000. Closing VA: 5% × 80 000 = 4 000. Change: -1 000)*

## Q3: January 2025 (6 pts — 6 questions)

**1.** Which account for booking cleaning of company's building?
- **Answer**: "Expenses for land and building"

**2.** Statement regarding accruals that applies?
- **Answer**: Accruals change the equity of the year

**3.** Production company pays due supplier invoice for supplies by bank transfer. Transaction type?
- **Answer**: Capital repayment *(reduces both asset and liability)*

**4.** Shareholders' capital and legal reserves no longer fully covered by losses brought forward?
- **Answer**: Excessive indebtedness *(Überschuldung)*

**5.** Company delivers goods and sends invoice. Effect on ratios?
- **Answer**: Quick ratio increases *(receivables increase, but no change in current liabilities)*

**6.** Company books a provision for outstanding legal proceeding. Effect on ratios?
- **Answer**: Equity ratio decreases *(liability increases, equity stays same, total assets increase)*

## Common Topics & Traps

**Transaction types** (appears every exam):
- **Asset swap**: One asset ↑, another ↓ (e.g., Bank → Receivable, Machine → Receivable)
- **Capital procurement**: Asset ↑ + Liability ↑ (e.g., buy van against invoice)
- **Capital repayment**: Asset ↓ + Liability ↓ (e.g., pay supplier by bank)
- **Liability swap**: One liability ↑, another ↓

**Financial ratio effects** (appears frequently):
- Sales on credit → Quick ratio increases (receivables ↑)
- Receiving goods with invoice → Current ratio decreases (current liabilities ↑)
- Booking provision → Equity ratio decreases (liabilities ↑)
- Leverage effect → ROE increases when ROA > interest rate

**Legal concepts**:
- **Loss of capital** (Kapitalverlust): Assets don't cover liabilities + 50% of equity
- **Excessive indebtedness** (Überschuldung): Shareholders' capital + reserves not covered

**Account classifications**:
- Building cleaning, insurance, mortgage interest → "Expenses for land and building"
- Owner takes goods → "Captive consumption"
- Unrealized land appreciation → NOT booked

**VA change calculation**: If expected default rate stays constant, VA changes proportionally with receivables balance
