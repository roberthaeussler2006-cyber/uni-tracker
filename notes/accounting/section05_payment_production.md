# Section 5: Payment Transactions & Production Company — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 9-10 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key differences from trading company:**
> - Production uses `Expenses for material` (not "Expenses for trading goods")
> - Production uses `Sales from produced goods` (not "Sales from trading goods")
> - Credit card: book to `Receivables from card organizations`, then settlement with commission
> - Prepayments: `Bank / Prepayments received` (liability until delivery)
> - Goods on own account: `[Asset] / Goods and services on own account` (at production cost)

## Q1: January 2026 (10 pts) — Flint Ltd. (chocolate production)

**Booking entries for the chocolate production Flint Ltd.:**

1. Customer *Judith Pratt* buys pralines in the factory store and pays CHF 80 by credit card.
2. The credit card company pays CHF 76 to the bank account of *Flint Ltd.*
3. *Corti Ltd.* delivers chocolate-grade milk powder to *Flint Ltd.*, valued at CHF 6 000, with invoice attached.
4. The next day, after deducting 2% trade discount, *Flint Ltd.* pays the invoice of *Corti Ltd.* by bank transfer.
5. *Flint Ltd.* plans for a new cupboard for the factory shop. *Flint Ltd.* asks a specialty carpenter for an offer. 2 days later the carpenter sends the offer with a price of CHF 3 000 per cupboard.
6. *Flint Ltd.* delivers various chocolate products to *Teicher Ltd.*, which owns various specialty stores. The total invoice amount is CHF 13 000.
7. When checking the delivery, *Teicher Ltd.* counts that not all products were delivered. They agree to lower the payment by CHF 500.
8. The delivery company sends an invoice of CHF 400 to *Flint Ltd.* for various deliveries to customers.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Receivables from card organizations / Sales from produced goods — 80**
2. Financial expenses / Receivables from card organizations — 4 *(commission: 80-76)*
   Bank / Receivables from card organizations — 76
3. **Expenses for material / Payables for goods and services — 6 000**
4. Payables for goods and services / Expenses for material — 120 *(2% trade discount)*
   Payables for goods and services / Bank — 5 880
5. **--- (no booking entry)** *(receiving an offer is not a transaction!)*
6. **Receivables from goods and services / Sales from produced goods — 13 000**
7. **Sales from produced goods / Receivables from goods and services — 500**
8. **Sales from produced goods / Payables for goods and services — 400** *(delivery to customers = outbound freight)*

</details>

## Q2: March 2025 (10 pts) — Sokia (smartphone production)

**Booking entries for the smartphone producer Sokia:**

1. Purchase of various raw materials (iron, magnesium, plastic, glass) against invoice, for CHF 47 000.
2. The purchase value of the magnesium is CHF 4 000. 10% shows contamination above the agreed threshold and is immediately sent back to the supplier.
3. *Moonrise* purchases 100 smartphones from *Sokia*, at a total value of CHF 40 000, against invoice.
4. *Moonrise* pays the invoice after 5 days by bank transfer, deducting a 5% quantity discount and a 2% trade discount.
5. A trader buys a smartphone in *Sokia's* online shop for CHF 5 000 and pays by credit card.
6. Following the above, the credit card organization pays CHF 4 750 to *Sokia's* bank account.
7. For an advertising campaign, *Sokia* produces a special edition smartphone, total production costs CHF 8 000.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Expenses for material / Payables for goods and services — 47 000**
2. **Payables for goods and services / Expenses for material — 400** *(10% of 4 000)*
3. **Receivables from goods and services / Sales from produced goods — 40 000**
4. Sales from produced goods / Receivables — 2 000 *(5% quantity discount)*
   Sales from produced goods / Receivables — 760 *(2% trade discount on remaining 38 000)*
   Bank / Receivables — 37 240
5. **Receivables from card organizations / Sales from produced goods — 5 000**
6. Financial expenses / Receivables from card organizations — 250 *(commission: 5 000 - 4 750)*
   Bank / Receivables from card organizations — 4 750
7. **Expenses for advertising / Goods and services on own account — 8 000** *(produced for own advertising use)*

</details>

</details>

## Q4: June 2024 (9 pts) — Christina Haupt (carpenter, kitchen production)

**Booking entries for the sole proprietorship of carpenter Christina Haupt:**

1. Ordered to produce a custom kitchen for CHF 87 000. Payment: 1/3 upon signing, 1/3 upon delivery, 1/3 after 30 days. What is the booking upon signing?
2. After 8 weeks, the kitchen is delivered. The customer pays the second third accordingly.
3. Discussions for a further kitchen project progress well. *Christina Haupt* sends an offer for CHF 35 000 which includes all appliances.
4. The supplier of screws fills all empty supply boxes as ordered and sends an invoice of CHF 2 300.
5. *Christina Haupt* pays the screw supplier by bank transfer, deducting 5% trade discount.
6. Quality issues with handles from a kitchen project from last year. Agrees on a price discount of CHF 1 400 and pays by bank.
7. After 5 years, *Christina Haupt* replaces the kitchen in the show room. Production costs for the new kitchen: CHF 22 300.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Bank / Prepayments received — 29 000** *(1/3 of 87 000)*
2. Three entries:
   Receivables from goods and services / Sales from produced goods — 87 000
   Prepayments received / Receivables from goods and services — 29 000 *(apply prepayment)*
   Bank / Receivables from goods and services — 29 000 *(second third payment)*
3. **--- (no booking entry)** *(sending an offer is not a transaction)*
4. **Expenses for supplies / Payables for goods and services — 2 300**
5. Payables for goods and services / Expenses for supplies — 115 *(5% discount)*
   Payables for goods and services / Bank — 2 185
6. **Extraordinary expenses / Bank — 1 400** *(quality issue from prior year = extraordinary)*
7. **Furniture and facilities / Goods and services on own account — 22 300** *(produced kitchen for own showroom)*

</details>

## Q5: January 2024 (9 pts) — 3D/PP (model architect)

## Patterns & Traps

- **Credit card**: Always 2 entries — (1) sale: `Rec. card organizations / Sales`, (2) settlement: `Bank / Rec. card org.` + `Financial expenses / Rec. card org.` for commission
- **Prepayments**: `Bank / Prepayments received` (liability). On delivery: create full sale, offset prepayment, collect rest.
- **Goods on own account**: Always at **production cost**, not sales value! `[Asset or Expense] / Goods and services on own account`
- **Offer/quotation**: NO booking entry (not a transaction yet)
- **Materials vs supplies**: Materials = main production inputs. Supplies = secondary items (screws, packaging)
- **Outbound freight**: `Sales from produced goods / Payables` (reduces net sales, just like trading company)
- **Quality issues from prior years**: `Extraordinary expenses / Bank` (not regular operating expense)
