# Section 6: Changes in Inventories — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 4 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key rules:**
> - **Trading goods, raw materials, supplies** (class 4 expense accounts):
>   - Inventory increased: `Inventory / Expense account` (reduces expense)
>   - Inventory decreased: `Expense account / Inventory` (increases expense)
> - **Semi-finished & finished goods** (account 3900):
>   - Inventory increased: `Inventory / Changes in inventories` (income)
>   - Inventory decreased: `Changes in inventories / Inventory` (reduces income)
> - Always **4 entries**, always **4 points**

## Q1: January 2026 (4 pts)

The following inventory values before booking changes in inventories are known (CHF):

| | Opening Jan 1 | Closing Dec 31 |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory, Trading Goods | 16 000 | 21 000 |
| Inventory, Material | 14 000 | 17 000 |
| Inventory, semi-finished goods | 9 000 | 5 000 |
| Inventory, finished goods | 22 000 | 28 000 |

What are the booking entries for the changes in inventories as per December 31?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Inventory, Trading Goods / Expenses for Trading Goods — 5 000** *(increased: 21 000 - 16 000)*
2. **Inventory, Material / Expenses for Material — 3 000** *(increased: 17 000 - 14 000)*
3. **Changes in inventories / Inventory, semi-finished goods — 4 000** *(decreased: 9 000 - 5 000)*
4. **Inventory, finished goods / Changes in inventories — 6 000** *(increased: 28 000 - 22 000)*

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Two different systems**: Trading goods/materials/supplies use their **expense account**; semi-finished/finished goods use **Changes in inventories** (account 3900)
- **Direction logic**: Inventory UP = debit inventory, credit the counterpart. Inventory DOWN = debit counterpart, credit inventory
- **Never mix systems**: Don't book trading goods through "Changes in inventories" or finished goods through an expense account
- **Always exactly 4 entries, 4 points** — one per inventory category in the table
- **Simple arithmetic**: Change = Closing - Opening. No rounding, no percentages
