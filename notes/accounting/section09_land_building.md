# Section 9: Land and Building — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 4-5 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key accounts:**
> - Asset: `Land and building` (1600)
> - Income: `Income from land and building` (7500)
> - Expense: `Expenses for land and building` (7510)
> - Own rent: `Lease expenses / Income from land and building`
>
> **Renovation split rule:**
> - Value-increasing part → capitalize to asset: `Land and building / Liabilities`
> - Value-maintaining part → expense: `Expenses for land and building / Liabilities`

## Q1: January 2026 (5 pts) — FerroTech Ltd. (machine production)

FerroTech owns several buildings and land. Booking entries:

1. Old windows replaced for improved thermal insulation. Invoice CHF 90 000 — 2/3 value-increasing, 1/3 value-maintaining.
2. Bank sends payment confirmation for CHF 2 000 rent payment of tenant Vanessa Vaal.
3. On December 31, FerroTech books renting garages for business vehicles in its own mixed-use building at an annual value of CHF 6 000.
4. In the same building, the company's caretaker rents an apartment for a reduced rent of CHF 9 000/year, paid on December 31.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Land and building / Misc. current liabilities — 60 000** *(value-increasing: 2/3 x 90 000)*
   **Expenses for land and building / Misc. current liabilities — 30 000** *(value-maintaining: 1/3 x 90 000)*
2. **Bank / Income from land and building — 2 000**
3. **Lease expenses / Income from land and building — 6 000** *(own rent — Eigenmiete)*
4. **Bank / Income from land and building — 9 000** *(tenant rent, even if reduced)*

</details>

## Q2: March 2025 (4 pts)

1. The company sells land and building at CHF 750 000 by bank transfer. Book value is CHF 800 000.
2. A consulting company rents additional office space at CHF 3 000/month. Pays first month by bank transfer. (Booking from the tenant's perspective.)
3. A company books the monthly rent of an office in its own building of CHF 8 500 for March.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Bank / Land and building — 750 000** *(sale proceeds)*
   **Expenses for land and building / Land and building — 50 000** *(loss: book value 800 000 - sale price 750 000)*
2. **Lease expenses / Bank — 3 000** *(tenant paying rent)*
3. **Lease expenses / Income from land and building — 8 500** *(own rent)*

</details>

## Q3: January 2025 (4 pts) — WhiteHorse (restaurant)

3. WhiteHorse books own rent of CHF 66 000.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

   **Expenses for land and building / Misc. current liabilities — 90 000** *(value-maintaining: 75% x 120 000)*
2. **Expenses for land and building / Bank — 10 000** *(mortgage interest)*
3. **Lease expenses / Income from land and building — 66 000** *(own rent)*

</details>

## Q4: March 2024 (4 pts) — Colorful (painting company)

Colorful owns a building: lower 2 floors for own use (showroom, storage, offices), 3rd floor has 2 apartments rented to Willi Weiss and Rosi Roth.

1. Rosi Roth pays rent of CHF 1 480 by bank transfer.
2. Bathroom in Willi Weiss's apartment renovated. Plumber's invoice: CHF 12 100. Colorful did the painting work itself: CHF 1 300.
3. At year end, Colorful considers its own rent of CHF 42 000.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Bank / Income from land and building — 1 480**
2. **Expenses for land and building / Misc. current liabilities — 12 100** *(plumber)*
   **Expenses for land and building / Goods and services on own account — 1 300** *(own painting work)*
3. **Lease expenses / Income from land and building — 42 000** *(own rent)*

</details>

## Q5: June 2024 (5 pts) — CSF Ltd. (building management company)

CSF Ltd. owns and manages two buildings (core business). Buying/selling of property is NOT core business.

1. Both buildings cleaned monthly by external company, CHF 3 200/month. Sends invoice for January and February at end of February.
2. Special room for server hardware added to one building. Construction invoice: CHF 187 000.
3. Office rooms newly painted. Painting company invoice: CHF 67 000.
4. Tenant Miller Ltd. pays monthly lease of CHF 3 480 by bank transfer.
5. At year end, CSF books own lease expenses of CHF 192 000.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Expenses for land and building / Payables for goods and services — 6 400** *(2 months x 3 200)*
2. **Land and building / Misc. current liabilities — 187 000** *(value-increasing: new construction)*
3. **Expenses for land and building / Payables for goods and services — 67 000** *(value-maintaining: repainting)*
4. **Bank / Income from land and building — 3 480**
5. **Lease expenses / Income from land and building — 192 000** *(own rent)*

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Own rent (Eigenmiete)**: Always `Lease expenses / Income from land and building` — appears in almost every exam
- **Value-increasing vs. value-maintaining**: New additions (server room, new windows) = capitalize to asset. Maintenance/repairs (painting, cleaning) = expense. When mixed, split by given percentage
- **Selling property below book value**: Two entries — (1) Bank / Land and building for sale price, (2) Expenses for L&B / Land and building for the loss amount
- **Own work on property**: `Expenses for land and building / Goods and services on own account`
- **Mortgage interest**: `Expenses for land and building / Bank`
- **Tenant rent always goes through**: `Bank / Income from land and building` (even reduced rent)
- **Don't confuse**: `Payables for goods and services` (for regular invoices) vs. `Misc. current liabilities` (for renovation/construction invoices)
