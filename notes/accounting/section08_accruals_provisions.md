# Section 8: Accruals and Provisions — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 5-8 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Four types of accruals (Transitorische):**
> 1. **Prepaid expense** (asset): Paid now, expense belongs to next year → `Accruals / Expense account`
> 2. **Accrued expense** (liability): Expense this year, invoice next year → `Expense account / Accruals`
> 3. **Accrued income** (asset): Revenue earned this year, invoice next year → `Accruals / Sales account`
> 4. **Deferred income** (liability): Payment received, revenue belongs to next year → `Sales account / Accruals`
>
> **Provisions:**
> - Create: `Other operating expenses / Provisions`
> - Dissolve (risk gone): `Provisions / Extraordinary income`
>
> **No booking entry**: Orders placed but goods not yet delivered

## Q1: January 2026 (5 pts) — JanzConsult Ltd. (software consulting)

Booking entries as per year-end closing December 31, 2025:

1. The office rent of CHF 4 600 for January and February 2026 has already been paid on December 28.
2. As a Christmas present, fast-charging devices have been given to all hotels in the surrounding area. The invoice of CHF 5 300 is expected for January 2026.

4. Because of a user mistake, the booking system of a customer must be reconfigured on December 28, 2025. The invoice of CHF 700 will only be sent next year.
5. In 2024, provisions of CHF 8 000 were booked for pending legal cases related to a software bug. The judge has ruled in favor of JanzConsult — provision is no longer needed.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Accruals / Lease expenses — 4 600** *(prepaid expense: rent paid for next year's months)*
2. **Advertising expenses / Accruals — 5 300** *(accrued expense: service this year, invoice next year)*
3. **Expenses for wages and salaries / Accruals — 9 000** *(accrued expense: bonus earned this year, paid next year)*
4. **Accruals / Sales from services — 700** *(accrued income: work done this year, invoice next year)*
5. **Provisions / Extraordinary income — 8 000** *(dissolution: risk no longer exists)*

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Time proportion calculations**: Always calculate months belonging to current vs. next year (e.g., paid April 1 for 12 months → 9 months current year, 3 months next year)
- **Mortgage interest accrual**: Count months since last payment date to Dec 31 (e.g., last paid Sept 30 → 3 months accrued: Oct, Nov, Dec)
- **"No booking" trap**: Orders/quotations where goods not yet delivered → no entry
- **Provision creation**: Always `Other operating expenses / Provisions` (lawsuits, damages, fines)
- **Provision dissolution**: Always `Provisions / Extraordinary income` (risk no longer exists)
- **Deferred income vs. Accrued income**: Payment received for next year → debit Sales / credit Accruals. Revenue earned but invoice next year → debit Accruals / credit Sales
- **Accruals is ONE account** used for both assets and liabilities (Transitorische Aktiven & Passiven)
