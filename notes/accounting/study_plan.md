## Study Schedule (Mar 3 - Mar 13)

### Day 1-2: Mar 3-4 — Sections 1 + 2 + 6 ← YOU ARE HERE
**Trading Company (13 pts) + Sole Proprietorship (3-4 pts) + Changes in Inventories (4 pts) = ~21 pts**

#### Section 1: Trading Company (13 pts)
*Part A: Booking entries (9 pts) + Part B: T-account calculations (4 pts)*

- [ ] Trading company model: purchasing, selling, inventory
- [ ] Trade discounts (Skonto) & quantity discounts (purchase & sale side)
- [ ] Captive consumption → **always at PURCHASE value** (they give selling price to trick you)
- [ ] Transport costs split: freight IN = Expenses for trading goods, freight OUT = Sales from trading goods
- [ ] "No booking" traps: ordering goods, replacing defective items, private orders, free goodwill gifts
- [ ] T-account calculations: PVGP, PVGS, gross profit margin, gross profit mark-up
- [ ] Inventory correction at year-end (increase vs decrease)
- [ ] Unbooked items at year-end (transport invoices, sales reductions)
- [ ] **Practice file**: `trading_company_practice.md` (12 Part A + 12 Part B from past papers)

#### Section 2: Sole Proprietorship (3-4 pts)
*Calculate equity, owner's income, close Private account*

- [ ] Private account: salary, interest, cash withdrawals
- [ ] Owner's income = salary + interest for owner - loss (or + profit)
- [ ] Equity at year-end = opening equity + increase - private surplus + profit
- [ ] Closing the Private account at year-end: `Private / Equity` or `Equity / Private`
- [ ] What goes through Private: owner salary, owner interest, cash withdrawals, captive consumption

#### Section 6: Changes in Inventories (4 pts)
*Inventory adjustment bookings for trading + production companies*

- [ ] Trading: change in inventory of trading goods
- [ ] Production: changes in semi-finished goods, finished goods
- [ ] Inventory increase = credit Expenses / debit Inventory
- [ ] Inventory decrease = debit Expenses / credit Inventory
- [ ] Year-end physical count vs book value

### Day 3-4: Mar 5-6 — Sections 5 + 7
**Payment Transactions & Production (9-10 pts) + Effect on Profit & Cash (4 pts) = ~14 pts**

#### Section 5: Payment Transactions & Production Company (9-10 pts)
*Cash, bank, credit card, prepayments, anticipatory tax, production model*

- [ ] Cash difference and petty cash
- [ ] Credit card payments (3% commission = financial expense)
- [ ] Prepayments: to suppliers = asset (Prepayments paid), from customers = liability (Prepayments received)
- [ ] Anticipatory tax (Verrechnungssteuer) in Switzerland (35%)
  - [ ] Interest/dividend received: Bank gets 65%, Receivables anticipatory tax gets 35%
- [ ] Bank account interest bookings (interest income, interest expense)
- [ ] Production company model:
  - [ ] Purchasing side: Expenses for materials, raw materials
  - [ ] Selling side: Sales from produced goods
  - [ ] Produced goods in stock: semi-finished, finished goods
  - [ ] Expenses for materials, changes in inventory
  - [ ] Goods and services on own account (captive consumption in production)
- [ ] Compiling year-end financial statements for production company

#### Section 7: Effect on Profit and Effect on Cash (4 pts)
*For each transaction: does it affect profit? does it affect cash?*

- [ ] Profit effect: Does it change an income or expense account?
- [ ] Cash effect: Does Cash or Bank account change?
- [ ] Common combos:
  - [ ] Sale on invoice: profit YES, cash NO
  - [ ] Customer pays invoice: profit NO, cash YES
  - [ ] Cash sale: profit YES, cash YES
  - [ ] Buy asset on invoice: profit NO, cash NO
  - [ ] Depreciation: profit YES (expense), cash NO
  - [ ] Accrual booking: profit YES, cash NO
  - [ ] Loan repayment: profit NO, cash YES

### Day 5: Mar 7 — Section 3
**Depreciation & Losses from Receivables (9-12 pts)**

#### Section 3: Depreciation & Losses from Receivables (9-12 pts)
*Sometimes split into two sub-sections, sometimes combined*

**Depreciation:**
- [ ] Depreciation concept: systematic planned value reduction of non-current assets
- [ ] Direct depreciation: `Depreciation / Asset account` (reduces asset directly)
- [ ] Indirect depreciation: `Depreciation / VA, Asset account` (uses value adjustment account)
- [ ] Straight-line method: Purchase value / useful life = annual depreciation
- [ ] Declining balance method: Rate × **book value** (decreasing amounts each year)
- [ ] Depreciation of **book value** vs **purchase value** — read carefully which one!
- [ ] Land + Building depreciation: `Expenses for land and building / VA, land and building`
- [ ] Selling non-current assets:
  - [ ] Sale above book value → Extraordinary income (profit on sale)
  - [ ] Sale below book value → Extraordinary expense (loss on sale)

**Losses from receivables:**
- [ ] Customer pays less than invoiced: `Losses from receivables / Receivables from G&S`
- [ ] Customer defaults completely: `Losses from receivables / Receivables from G&S`
- [ ] Debt collection process:
  - [ ] Start process: `Receivables from G&S / Bank` (pay fee to government)
  - [ ] Successful: `Bank / Receivables from G&S` (receive money + fee + late interest)
  - [ ] Partially successful: book what's received, write off rest as loss
  - [ ] Unsuccessful: write off full amount as loss
- [ ] Sending a reminder → **no booking entry!**
- [ ] Expected losses from receivables (year-end adjustment):
  - [ ] `Losses from receivables / VA, receivables from G&S` (increase)
  - [ ] or `VA, receivables / Losses from receivables` (decrease)
- [ ] Debt certificate received years later: `Bank / Extraordinary income`

### Day 6-7: Mar 8-9 — Sections 8 + 9 + 10
**Accruals & Provisions (5-6 pts) + Land & Building (4-5 pts) + Valuation Reserves (4-5 pts) = ~14 pts**

#### Section 8: Accruals and Provisions (5-6 pts)
*The 4 accrual cases + provisions*

- [ ] **The 4 accrual cases** (critical — memorize the pattern!):
  - [ ] **Post-paid expense** (expense THIS year, pay NEXT year)
    - Dec 31: `Expense / Accrued liabilities and deferred income`
    - Jan 1: reversal
  - [ ] **Post-paid revenue** (income THIS year, receive NEXT year)
    - Dec 31: `Accrued income / Income`
    - Jan 1: reversal
  - [ ] **Pre-paid expense** (pay THIS year, expense NEXT year)
    - Dec 31: `Prepayments paid / Expense`
    - Jan 1: reversal
  - [ ] **Pre-paid revenue** (receive THIS year, income NEXT year)
    - Dec 31: `Income / Prepayments received`
    - Jan 1: reversal
- [ ] Accrual bookings on Dec 31 + automatic reversal Jan 1
- [ ] Provisions: existing obligation, uncertain timing/amount
  - [ ] Create: `Expense / Provisions`
  - [ ] Use: `Provisions / Bank (or Payables)`
  - [ ] Dissolve unused: `Provisions / Extraordinary income`
- [ ] Accruals vs provisions comparison

#### Section 9: Land and Building (4-5 pts)
*Mixed use property: business + private*

- [ ] Asset account: Land and buildings
- [ ] Income account: Income from land and buildings (rental income)
- [ ] Expense account: Expenses for land and buildings (maintenance, mortgage interest)
- [ ] Private use portion: `Expenses for land and building / Private` or `Private / Income from land and building`
- [ ] Depreciation: `Expenses for land and building / VA, land and building`
- [ ] Profit from sale = `Bank / Land and building` + `Land and building / Extraordinary income`
- [ ] Discontinuing a business segment

#### Section 10: Valuation Reserves (4-5 pts)
*External reporting vs internal reality*

- [ ] Concept: external book value ≠ internal actual value
- [ ] Generating valuation reserves (3 ways):
  - [ ] Undervaluing assets (reporting lower than actual)
  - [ ] Overvaluing liabilities (reporting higher than actual)
  - [ ] Immediate one-off depreciation (extra depreciation beyond planned)
- [ ] Dissolving valuation reserves (3 ways):
  - [ ] Reduce excess depreciation
  - [ ] Reverse overvalued liabilities
  - [ ] Sell undervalued asset at true value
- [ ] Actual profit vs reported profit: actual = reported + created reserves - dissolved reserves
- [ ] Legal basis in Switzerland (Art. 960a CO)
- [ ] Exam twist: "By what amount would gross profit change if inventory disclosed at X% of internal value?"

### Day 8: Mar 10 — Section 11
**Marketable Securities (10-13 pts) — second highest section!**

#### Section 11: Marketable Securities (10-13 pts)
*Bonds, shares, purchase, sale, dividends, year-end valuation*

- [ ] Three asset classes by investment objective:
  - [ ] Short-term marketable securities (trading)
  - [ ] Financial assets (medium-term)
  - [ ] Investments in subsidiaries (long-term)
- [ ] **Bonds:**
  - [ ] Nominal value, current value (market price), interest rate, maturity
  - [ ] Accrued interest (Marchzins): interest earned since last coupon date
  - [ ] Purchase: `Securities + Accrued interest / Bank` (price + accrued interest + commission)
  - [ ] Interest received: `Bank / Financial income` (full coupon)
  - [ ] Sale: `Bank / Securities + Accrued interest` (price + accrued interest - commission)
- [ ] **Shares:**
  - [ ] Market price, nominal value
  - [ ] Purchase: `Securities / Bank` (price + commission)
  - [ ] Dividend received: split by anticipatory tax:
    - [ ] `Bank / Financial income` — 65% of gross dividend
    - [ ] `Receivables, anticipatory tax / Financial income` — 35% of gross dividend
  - [ ] Sale: `Bank / Securities` (price - commission), book profit/loss
- [ ] **Year-end valuation:**
  - [ ] Lower of cost or market (imparity principle)
  - [ ] Direct method: adjust asset account directly
  - [ ] Indirect method: use value adjustment account
- [ ] Reversing accrual bookings (Jan 1 of next year)

### Day 9: Mar 11 — Sections 12 + 13 + 14
**Joint-Stock Company (4-6 pts) + Financial Ratios (6-7 pts) + P&L Reporting (4 pts) = ~15 pts**

#### Section 12: Joint-Stock Company (4-6 pts)
*Founding, capital increases, profit distribution, loss scenarios*

- [ ] Founding: `Bank / Shareholders' capital` + `Non paid-in share capital / Shareholders' capital`
- [ ] Capital increase: new shares at premium → Legal capital reserves (share premium)
- [ ] **Profit distribution flow** (Art. 671 OR):
  1. Start: profit of the period + profit brought forward
  2. First allocation: 5% to legal profit reserves (until reserve = 50% of paid-in capital)
  3. Base dividend: 5% of paid-in capital
  4. Second allocation: 10% of surplus dividend to legal profit reserves
  5. Surplus dividend (additional dividend)
  6. Profit sharing bonus (Tantieme)
  7. Remainder → profit brought forward
- [ ] Dividend payment with anticipatory tax:
  - [ ] 65% paid to shareholder: `Profit distributions / Bank`
  - [ ] 35% to government: `Profit distributions / Payables, anticipatory tax`
- [ ] Loss scenarios: loss, significant loss (>50% of capital), excessive loss (liabilities > assets)
- [ ] Nominal value repayment vs dividends

#### Section 13: Financial Ratios (6-7 pts)
*Liquidity, leverage, profitability ratios*

- [ ] **Liquidity ratios:**
  - [ ] Cash ratio = Cash / Current liabilities
  - [ ] Quick ratio = (Cash + Receivables) / Current liabilities
  - [ ] Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
- [ ] **Leverage ratios:**
  - [ ] Equity ratio = Equity / Total assets
  - [ ] Debt ratio = Liabilities / Total assets
  - [ ] Long-term debt to non-current asset ratio = (Equity + Non-current liabilities) / Non-current assets
- [ ] **Profitability ratios:**
  - [ ] Return on equity (ROE) = Profit / Equity
  - [ ] Return on assets (ROA) = (Profit + Interest expense) / Total assets
  - [ ] Return on sales (ROS) = Profit / Net sales
  - [ ] Gross profit margin = Gross profit / Net sales
- [ ] Know which direction is "good" for each ratio
- [ ] Calculate from statement of financial position / P&L data

#### Section 14: Statement of P&L in Reporting Format (4 pts)
*Classify accounts into the P&L waterfall*

- [ ] The P&L waterfall (memorize the structure!):
  ```
  3000-3999  Sales from goods and services
  - 4000-4999  Material and goods expenses
  = Gross income
  - 5000-5999  Personnel expenses
  - 6000-6799  Other operating expenses
  = EBITDA
  - 6800-6899  Depreciation and Amortization
  = EBIT
  +/- 6900-6999  Financial results
  = EBT
  +/- 7000-7999  Accessory operating result
  +/- 8000-8899  Extraordinary and non-operating result
  = Profit before taxes
  - 8900-8999  Taxes
  = Profit of the period
  ```
- [ ] Given a list of account balances → slot each into the correct line → calculate each subtotal
- [ ] Watch for: Captive consumption (income side, 3710), Changes in inventory (3900), Losses from receivables (3805)

### Day 10: Mar 12 — Sections 4 + 15 + 16
**Interpretation (4 pts) + Miscellaneous (6 pts) + Board Decisions (6-8 pts) = ~16 pts**

#### Section 4: Interpretation of Booking Entries (4 pts)
*Given a booking entry → describe the business transaction in words*

- [ ] Read Debit / Credit and identify what type of transaction it represents
- [ ] Common patterns to recognize:
  - [ ] `Expenses for trading goods / Payables` = goods delivery received on invoice
  - [ ] `Payables / Expenses for trading goods` + `Payables / Bank` = payment with trade discount
  - [ ] `Private / Captive consumption` = owner takes goods for private use
  - [ ] `Depreciation / VA, machines` = indirect depreciation of machines
  - [ ] `Losses from receivables / Receivables` = customer default/write-off
  - [ ] `Provisions / Bank` = using a provision to pay an obligation
  - [ ] `Accrued income / Financial income` = post-paid revenue accrual
- [ ] Practice: go through past paper "Interpretation" sections

#### Section 15: Miscellaneous (6 pts)
*Grab-bag of smaller topics that don't fit elsewhere*

- [ ] Corrections/reversals of wrong bookings
- [ ] VAT basics (if tested)
- [ ] Extraordinary income/expenses classification
- [ ] Account classification questions (is it asset/liability/income/expense?)
- [ ] Mixed questions combining multiple topics
- [ ] This section varies most between exams — best prepared by doing past papers

#### Section 16: Board of Director Decisions (6-8 pts)
*Multi-year analysis and strategic decisions*

- [ ] Multi-year business transaction consequences
- [ ] Make or buy decisions (compare costs over multiple years)
- [ ] CapEx vs leasing (compare total cost including depreciation, interest)
- [ ] Board of directors decision analysis:
  - [ ] Status quo vs diversification/expansion
  - [ ] Calculate financial impact of each option over multiple years
  - [ ] Consider: revenue changes, cost changes, investment needed, depreciation impact
- [ ] Usually involves a table comparing scenarios with numbers
- [ ] Apply financial ratios to evaluate which option is better

## Exam Format Reminders

- **Account names only** (not numbers) from the chart of accounts
- **Full numbers**: no separators, no decimals → `10000` not `10'000`
- **Percentages**: 2 decimal places, no % sign → `45.67` not `45.67%`
- **No booking needed**: put a dash `–` in all 3 answer boxes
- **Allowed aids**: TI-30 calculator only, bilingual dictionary
- **Software**: LockDown Browser on laptop (no tablets)
