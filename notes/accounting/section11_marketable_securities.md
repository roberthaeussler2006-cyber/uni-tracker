# Section 11: Marketable Securities — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 10-13 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key formulas:**
> - **Buying shares**: Shares × market value + expenses
> - **Selling shares**: Shares × selling price - expenses
> - **Buying bonds**: Nominal × rate% + accrued interest + expenses
> - **Selling bonds**: Nominal × rate% + accrued interest - expenses
> - **Accrued interest**: Nominal × interest rate × days/360
> - **Dividend/Interest split**: Gross × 65% = net (Bank), Gross × 35% = withholding tax (Receivables tax authority)
> - **Gross dividend**: Shares × nominal value × dividend%

## Q1: January 2026 (11 pts) — Samuel Kuster (sole proprietorship)

At beginning of year: 40 shares of *Get-Go Ltd.*, nominal CHF 75, purchase value CHF 320, book value CHF 35/share.

| Date | Transaction |
|---|---|
| Jan 1 | Opening "Marketable Securities" |
| Mar 6 | Get-Go pays gross dividend of 60%, by bank transfer |
| Mar 18 | Selling 10 shares at CHF 360/share, expenses CHF 32 |
| Mar 30 | Buys CHF 30 000 bond of MWB Ltd., 2% interest on Jun 30, rate 104, expenses CHF 105 |
| Jun 30 | MWB Ltd. bond interest payment |
| Oct 31 | Sells half of MWB bond at rate 105, expenses CHF 90 |
| Dec 30 | Bank charges of CHF 85 |
| Dec 31 | Book accrued interest on remaining bond |
| Dec 31 | Close "Marketable Securities": Get-Go shares at CHF 370, MWB bond at rate 100 |

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

**Jan 1:** Marketable securities / Statement of financial position — 1 400 *(40 × 35)*

**Mar 6:** Bank / Financial income — 1 170 *(gross 40 × 75 × 60% = 1 800; net 65%)*
Receivables tax authority / Financial income — 630 *(35% withholding)*

**Mar 18:** Bank / Marketable securities — 3 568 *(10 × 360 - 32)*

**Mar 30:** Marketable securities / Bank — 31 800 *(30 000 × 1.04 + accrued interest + 105)*

**Jun 30:** Bank / Financial income — 390 *(30 000 × 2% = 600; net 65%)*
Receivables tax authority / Financial income — 210 *(35%)*

**Oct 31:** Bank / Marketable securities — 15 760 *(15 000 × 1.05 + accrued interest - 90)*

**Dec 30:** Financial expenses / Bank — 85

**Dec 31 accrual:** Accruals / Financial income — 150 *(15 000 × 2% × 180/360)*

**Dec 31 closing:** Statement of financial position / Marketable securities — 26 100 *(30 × 370 + 15 000 × 1.00 = 11 100 + 15 000)*

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Highest-weighted section** (10-13 pts) — always has Part A (bank calculations) + Part B (full booking entries)
- **Withholding tax split**: Always 35% to `Receivables tax authority / Financial income` and 65% to `Bank / Financial income`
- **Bond accrued interest**: Count days from last interest date using 30-day months (360-day year)
- **Jan 1 accruals**: Open the accrual, then IMMEDIATELY reverse it (`Financial income / Accruals`)
- **Buying bonds**: Rate + accrued interest + expenses (all DEBIT to Marketable securities)
- **Selling bonds**: Rate + accrued interest - expenses (all CREDIT from Marketable securities to Bank)
- **Buying shares**: Market value + expenses
- **Selling shares**: Selling price - expenses (the gain/loss is automatic — no separate booking)
- **Capital repayment** (rare): `Bank / Marketable securities` — reduces the securities account, NOT income
- **Year-end**: Close at market value using `SFP / Marketable securities`
- **Bank fees**: Always `Financial expenses / Bank`
