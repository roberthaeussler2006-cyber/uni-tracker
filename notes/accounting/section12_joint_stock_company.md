# Section 12: Joint-Stock Company (AG) — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 5-6 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key formulas:**
> - **Paid-in capital** = Shareholders' capital - Non-paid-in capital
> - **Profit for distribution** = Profit/Loss of year +/- Profit/Loss brought forward
> - **First reserve (Art. 671 para 1)**: 5% of annual profit → until reserves reach 20% of paid-in capital
> - **Second reserve (Art. 671 para 2)**: 10% of amounts distributed beyond 5% base dividend
> - **Dividend base** = paid-in capital, full percentage points only
> - **Loss year booking**: `Profit brought forward / Loss of the period`
> - **Profit year booking**: `Profit of the period / Profit brought forward`

## Q1: January 2026 (5 pts)

Balance sheet before profit distribution (CHF):

| Assets | | Equity & Liabilities | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Misc. current assets | 160 000 | Misc. liabilities | 220 000 |
| Misc. non-current assets | 620 000 | Shareholders' capital | 500 000 |
| Non paid-in share capital | 20 000 | Legal capital reserves | 40 000 |
| | | Legal profit reserves | 20 000 |
| | | Losses brought forward | -10 000 |
| | | Profit of the year | 30 000 |

Board suggests: offset losses as required, only legally required reserves, maximum dividend (full %).

Calculate: Profit for distribution, reserve allocation, gross dividend (CHF), profit brought forward.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

- **Paid-in capital**: 500 000 - 20 000 = **480 000**
- **Profit for distribution**: 30 000 - 10 000 = **20 000**
- **Reserves**: (40 000 + 20 000) / 480 000 = 12.5% < 20% → first reserve needed: 5% × 20 000 = **1 000**
- **Remaining**: 20 000 - 1 000 = 19 000
- **Dividend**: 3% × 480 000 = 14 400 (4% = 19 200 > 19 000) → **CHF 14 400 (3%)**
- **Second reserve**: Dividend 3% < 5% base → no second reserve
- **Profit brought forward**: 19 000 - 14 400 = **CHF 4 600**

</details>

## Q2: March 2025 (5 pts)

| Assets | | Equity & Liabilities | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Various current | 343 000 | Various liabilities | 390 000 |
| Various non-current | 520 000 | Shareholders' capital | 400 000 |
| Non-paid-in capital | 100 000 | Legal profit reserves | 23 000 |
| | | Legal capital reserves | 126 000 |
| | | Losses brought forward | -2 000 |
| | | Profit of the year | 26 000 |

Board suggests: losses only as legally required, only legal reserves, maximum dividend (full %).

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

- **Paid-in capital**: 400 000 - 100 000 = **300 000** (1% = 3 000)
- **Profit for distribution**: 26 000 - 2 000 = **24 000**
- **Reserves**: (23 000 + 126 000) / 300 000 = 49.7% > 20% → **no first reserve**
- **Dividend**: 7% × 300 000 = 21 000 → **CHF 21 000 (7%)**
- **Second reserve**: 7% > 5% → 10% × (7% × 300 000) = 10% × 21 000 = **2 100**
- **Check**: 21 000 + 2 100 = 23 100 < 24 000 → fits
- **Profit brought forward**: 24 000 - 21 000 - 2 100 = **CHF 900**

</details>

## Q3: January 2025 (6 pts)

| Assets | | Equity & Liabilities | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current | 321 600 | Liabilities | 415 500 |
| Non-current | 475 400 | Shareholders' capital | 300 000 |
| Non paid-in capital | 60 000 | Legal capital reserves | 39 700 |
| | | Legal profit reserves | 67 800 |
| | | Loss brought forward | -4 000 |
| | | Profit of the year | 38 000 |

Board suggests: losses only as required, only legal reserves, maximum dividend (full %).

What is the booking entry for profit allocation?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

- **Paid-in capital**: 300 000 - 60 000 = **240 000**
- **Profit for distribution**: 38 000 - 4 000 = **34 000**
- **Booking entry**: Profit of the period / Profit brought forward — **38 000**
- **Reserves**: (39 700 + 67 800) / 240 000 = 44.8% > 20% → no first reserve
- **5% base dividend**: 240 000 × 5% = 12 000
- **Surplus calculation**: Each 1% surplus = 2 400 + 240 (10% reserve) = 2 640
- **Max surplus**: (34 000 - 12 000) / 2 640 = 8.33 → 8% surplus
- **Total dividend**: 5% + 8% = **13%** × 240 000 = **CHF 31 200**
- **Legal reserves (second)**: 10% × 8% × 240 000 = 10% × 19 200 = **CHF 1 920**
- **Profit brought forward**: 34 000 - 31 200 - 1 920 = **CHF 880**

</details>

## Q4: March 2024 (6 pts) — LOSS YEAR

| Assets | | Equity & Liabilities | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current | 512 000 | Liabilities | 483 000 |
| Non-current | 527 000 | Shareholders' capital | 400 000 |
| Non paid-in capital | 80 000 | Legal capital reserve | 60 000 |
| | | Legal profit reserves | 110 000 |
| | | Voluntary profit reserves | 40 000 |
| | | Profit brought forward | 38 000 |
| | | **Loss of the year** | **-12 000** |

Board suggests: losses only as required, legal minimum reserves, maximum dividend (full %).

What is the booking entry for the loss?

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

- **Paid-in capital**: 400 000 - 80 000 = **320 000**
- **Profit for distribution**: 38 000 - 12 000 = **26 000**
- **Loss booking entry**: Profit brought forward / Loss of the period — **12 000**
- **Reserves**: (60 000 + 110 000) / 320 000 = 53.1% > 20% → no first reserve
- **No annual profit** → no 5% first reserve applies
- **Dividend**: 8% × 320 000 = 25 600 (fits in 26 000) → **CHF 25 600 (8%)**
- **Second reserve check**: 8% > 5%, second reserve = 10% × 3% × 320 000 = 960. But 25 600 + 960 = 26 560 > 26 000 → **cannot afford second reserve → CHF 0**
- **Reserve allocation**: **CHF 0**
- **Profit brought forward**: 26 000 - 25 600 = **CHF 400**

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Always check paid-in capital first**: Shareholders' capital minus non-paid-in
- **Loss years**: No annual profit → no 5% first reserve. Loss is offset against profit brought forward
- **Reserve threshold**: Compare (legal capital reserves + legal profit reserves) to 20% of paid-in capital
- **Second reserve**: Only applies when dividend exceeds 5% base. Can't pay second reserve if it would exceed available profit
- **Dividend always on paid-in capital**, not shareholders' capital
- **Full percentage points only**: If 7.8% would work, use 7%
- **Loss booking**: `Profit brought forward / Loss of the period`
- **Profit booking**: `Profit of the period / Profit brought forward`
- **50% rule** (Art. 671 para 3): If reserves reach 50% of *nominal* capital (not paid-in), additional rules may apply — hasn't been fully tested yet
