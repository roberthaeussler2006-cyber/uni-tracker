# Section 4: Interpretation of Booking Entries — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 4 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Two formats used:**
> - **Multiple choice** (2025-2026): Pick the correct statement about a given booking entry
> - **Open-ended** (2024 and earlier): Given a booking entry, describe the business transaction in words

---

## Q1: January 2026 (4 pts) — Multiple Choice

**Mark the applicable answer. Only one answer applies per question.**

**1.** The sole proprietorship Baker Bruno records: `Equity / Private CHF 5 000`

- a) The equity increases by CHF 5 000
- b) This is the year-end booking of a left-side surplus in "Private"
- c) This is the year-end booking of a right-side surplus in "Private"
- d) The company has recorded a profit in the last year
- e) None of the above

**2.** A company books: `Depreciation / VA, vehicles CHF 2 000`

- a) Declining balance calculation, booking method unclear
- b) Straight line calculation, booking method unclear
- c) Direct booking method, calculation method unclear
- d) Indirect booking method, calculation method unclear
- e) None of the above

**3.** A company books: `Non paid-in shareholders' capital / Shareholders' capital CHF 60 000`

- a) Shareholder's capital is increased by CHF 60 000
- b) Shareholder's capital is decreased by CHF 60 000
- c) Shareholder's capital of CHF 60 000 is paid in
- d) The equity remains unchanged
- e) None of the above

**4.** A company books: `Financial Expenses / Bank account CHF 2 000`. Which does NOT apply?

- a) This might be the booking of interest for a bank loan
- b) This might be the booking of bank charges
- c) This might be the booking of mortgage interest
- d) All of the above apply
- e) None of the above

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **b)** Left-side surplus = owner took more than earned, close with Equity/Private
2. **d)** VA account = indirect method. Cannot tell calculation method from the entry alone
3. **a)** Debit Non paid-in (reduces it) / Credit Shareholders' capital (increases it) = capital increase
4. **c)** Mortgage interest would be booked to Loans account, not Bank. (Mortgages are long-term liabilities, interest is paid from Bank but the account "Financial Expenses / Bank" is correct for bank loans and charges)

</details>

## Q2: March 2025 (4 pts) — Multiple Choice

**1.** The owner of a small real estate company increases equity by bringing 2 standing lamps (value CHF 800 each) into the company. What is the booking entry?

- a) Furniture and facilities / Misc. current liabilities CHF 1 600
- b) Equity / Furniture and facilities CHF 1 600
- c) Misc. current liabilities / Furniture and facilities CHF 1 600
- d) Furniture and facilities / Equity CHF 1 600
- e) None of the above

**2.** On December 15, 2024, an IT consulting company receives CHF 8 000 for services to be provided in January 2025. How is this booked in 2024?

- a) Accruals / Sales from services CHF 8 000
- b) Accruals / Expenses for IT CHF 8 000
- c) Sales from services / Accruals CHF 8 000
- d) Expenses for IT / Accruals CHF 8 000
- e) None of the above

**3.** On January 31, the drug store finds cash is CHF 20 short (CHF 9 380 instead of 9 400). Reason unclear. What is the correction entry?

- a) Other operating expenses / Cash CHF 20
- b) Cash / Expenses for trading goods CHF 20
- c) Depreciation / Cash CHF 20
- d) Cash / Financial expenses CHF 20
- e) None of the above

**4.** An industrial company faces a court case for a faulty product. Expected damage: CHF 22 000. What is the provision booking?

- a) Provisions / Extraordinary expenses CHF 22 000
- b) Sales from produced goods / Provisions CHF 22 000
- c) Provisions / Other operating expenses CHF 22 000
- d) Other personnel expenses / Provisions CHF 22 000
- e) None of the above

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **d)** Owner brings assets into company = equity contribution. Debit the asset, credit Equity.
2. **c)** Pre-paid revenue: received money for future service → must defer the revenue. Sales from services / Accruals (deferred income)
3. **a)** Cash shortage with unknown reason = other operating expense
4. **e) None** — The correct entry would be `Other operating expenses / Provisions CHF 22 000` but option c) has it reversed (Provisions / Other operating expenses). Since no option is correct, answer is "None."

</details>

## Q3: January 2025 (4 pts) — Open-ended

**Describe the business transaction that led to each booking entry. A third person must be able to conclude the booking entry from your description.**

1. `Furniture and facilities / Cash CHF 720`
2. `Statement of financial position / Statement of profit or loss CHF 5 800`
3. `Sales from trading goods / Cash CHF 240`
4. `Value Adjustment, vehicles / Vehicles CHF 42 000`

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Purchase of office furniture (e.g., a chair), paid in cash for CHF 720**
2. **Year-end closing: the loss of CHF 5 800 is transferred to equity** (Balance sheet absorbs the P&L loss)
3. **A customer returns a previously purchased and paid-for item; CHF 240 is refunded in cash**
4. **When selling an old vehicle, the accumulated indirect depreciation (VA) of CHF 42 000 is dissolved/reversed against the asset account**

</details>

## Q4: June 2024 (4 pts) — Open-ended

**Describe the business transaction:**

1. `Misc. Current liabilities / Furniture and facilities CHF 3 000`
2. `Expenses for land and building / Provisions CHF 100 000`
3. `Energy and waste disposal expenses / Misc. current liabilities CHF 1 700`
4. `Cash / Other income CHF 40`

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Returning faulty furniture to the supplier** (reduces liability and asset)
2. **A new law requires future renovation; a provision is created for the estimated costs**
3. **Annual invoice for waste disposal services received** (expense recognized, liability created)
4. **Cash received for non-core business income** (e.g., a tip or miscellaneous payment)

</details>

## Patterns & Tips

- **Multiple choice traps**: They often include one option with the debit/credit reversed — read carefully!
- **Open-ended format**: Be specific (e.g., "purchase of a chair" not "increase in assets"). Generic descriptions like "increase of equity" score fewer points.
- Common entries to recognize:
  - `Equity / Private` = closing left-side surplus of Private account
  - `Private / Equity` = closing right-side surplus of Private account
  - `Depreciation / VA, [asset]` = indirect depreciation
  - `VA, [asset] / [asset]` = dissolving VA when selling asset
  - `Sales / Cash` = customer return with cash refund
  - `[Expense] / Provisions` = creating a provision
  - `Bank / Prepayments received` = receiving prepayment from customer
