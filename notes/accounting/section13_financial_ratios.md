# Section 13: Financial Ratios — Practice Questions

**Avg exam weight: 6-7 pts | 5 past papers extracted**

> **Key formulas (all in %):**
> - **Quick ratio** = (Cash + Securities + Receivables) / Current liabilities
> - **Current ratio** = Current assets / Current liabilities
> - **Equity ratio** = Equity / Total assets
> - **Coverage ratio** = (Equity + Non-current liabilities) / Non-current assets
> - **ROE** = Profit / Average equity
> - **ROA** = (Profit + Financial expenses) / Average total assets
> - **Return on sales** = Profit / Net revenues
> - **EBITDA** = EBIT + Depreciation

## Q1: January 2026 (7 pts) — Watch industry company (million CHF)

**Balance sheet highlights (Dec 31, 2024):**
- Cash: 1 103 | Securities: 293 | Receivables: 612 | Total current assets: 10 006
- Current liabilities: 1 022 | Non-current liabilities: 753
- Non-current assets: 3 986 | Total assets: 13 992 | Equity: 12 217
- Prior year equity: 12 258

**P&L highlights (2024):** Profit: 219

Calculate for 2024: Quick ratio, Coverage ratio, Debt ratio, ROE. Then assess as good or mediocre.

<details>
<summary>Show Solutions</summary>

1. **Quick ratio**: (1 103 + 293 + 612) / 1 022 = **196.48%**
2. **Coverage ratio**: (753 + 12 217) / 3 986 = **325.39%**
3. **Debt ratio (= equity ratio)**: 12 217 / 13 992 = **87.31%**
4. **ROE**: 219 / ((12 217 + 12 258) / 2) = **1.79%**

**Assessment:**
- Quick ratio: **Good** (>100%)
- Debt ratio: **Good** (very high equity)
- ROE: **Mediocre** (very low return despite high equity)

</details>

## Patterns & Traps

- **Quick ratio**: Include Cash + Securities + Receivables + Other current receivables, but EXCLUDE inventories and accruals
- **ROE uses average equity**: (Opening + Closing) / 2 — but some exams use single-year equity (check wording)
- **ROA**: Add back financial expenses to profit in the numerator
- **Return on sales**: Denominator may include net sales + changes in inventory + other operating income (check what exam gives)
- **Coverage ratio**: Must be > 100% (golden rule of financing)
- **EBITDA**: EBIT + Depreciation (add back depreciation to EBIT)
- **All ratios to 2 decimal places** (e.g., 196.48%, not 196.5%)
- **Watch for "incl. minorities"** in equity — if minorities exist, include them
- **Assessment**: Quick ratio > 100% = good. Equity ratio > 50% = good. ROE depends on context
